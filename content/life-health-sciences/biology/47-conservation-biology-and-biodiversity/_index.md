In the 1980s, biologists working in Lake Victoria in Africa discovered
one of the most extraordinary products of evolution on the planet.
Located in the Great Rift Valley, Lake Victoria is an enormous and deep
lake about 68,900 km² in area (larger than Lake Huron, the second
largest of North America's Great Lakes). Biologists were studying
species of a family of fish called cichlids. When they sampled for fish
in different locations of the lake, the researchers identified over 500
evolved species in total. However, the scientists soon discovered that
the invasive Nile Perch was destroying the lake's cichlid population,
bringing hundreds of cichlid species to extinction with devastating
rapidity.

## Sections

- **The Biodiversity Crisis** — biodiversity in terms of species
  diversity and abundance, biodiversity as the equilibrium of naturally
  fluctuating rates of extinction and speciation, and historical causes
  of high extinction rates in Earth's history.
- **The Importance of Biodiversity to Human Life** — chemical diversity
  benefits to humans, biodiversity components that support human
  agriculture, and ecosystem services.
- **Threats to Biodiversity** — significant threats to biodiversity,
  the effects of habitat loss, the introduction of exotic species, and
  hunting, and the early and predicted effects of climate change on
  biodiversity.
- **Preserving Biodiversity** — new technologies and methods for
  describing biodiversity, the legislative framework for conservation,
  principles and challenges of conservation preserve design, examples
  of the effects of habitat restoration, and the role of zoos in
  biodiversity conservation.
