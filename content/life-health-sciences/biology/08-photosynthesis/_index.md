The metabolic processes in all organisms—from bacteria to humans—require
energy. To get this energy, many organisms access stored energy by eating,
that is, by ingesting other organisms. But where does the stored energy in
food originate? All of this energy can be traced back to photosynthesis.

## Sections

- **Overview of Photosynthesis** — why photosynthesis matters to other
  living organisms, the main structures involved, and its substrates and
  products.
- **The Light-Dependent Reactions of Photosynthesis** — how plants absorb
  energy from sunlight, short and long wavelengths of light, and where
  within a plant photosynthesis takes place.
- **Using Light Energy to Make Organic Molecules** — the Calvin cycle,
  carbon fixation, and photosynthesis's place in the energy cycle of all
  living things.
