{{< selfcheck question="Why does nitrogen's triple covalent bond make it hard for living organisms to use nitrogen gas from the atmosphere?" >}}
Molecular nitrogen (N₂) consists of two nitrogen atoms joined by a triple covalent bond, and the more covalent bonds between two atoms, the stronger their connection, so triple bonds are the strongest. This strong triple bond makes it difficult for living systems to break apart the nitrogen for use as a constituent of proteins and DNA.

{{< selfcheck question="Why does water's oxygen atom carry a partial negative charge while its hydrogen atoms carry a partial positive charge?" >}}
Water is polar because oxygen is more electronegative than hydrogen, so the electrons shared in its covalent bonds spend more time near the oxygen nucleus than near the hydrogen nuclei. This generates a partial negative charge near the oxygen and a partial positive charge near the hydrogens.

different organisms that scientists have sequenced to date, 37 of these
amino acids appear in the same position in all cytochrome c samples.
