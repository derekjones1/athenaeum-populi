Viewed from space, Earth offers no clues about the diversity of life forms
that reside there. Scientists believe that the first forms of life on Earth
were microorganisms that existed for billions of years in the ocean before
plants and animals appeared. The mammals, birds, and flowers so familiar to
us are all relatively recent, originating 130 to 250 million years ago. The
earliest representatives of the genus *Homo*, to which we belong, have
inhabited this planet for only the last 2.5 million years, and only in the
last 300,000 years have humans started looking like we do today.
