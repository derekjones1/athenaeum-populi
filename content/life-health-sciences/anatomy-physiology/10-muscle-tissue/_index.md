When most people think of muscles, they think of the muscles that are visible
just under the skin, particularly of the limbs. These are skeletal muscles,
so-named because most of them move the skeleton. But there are two other types
of muscle in the body, with distinctly different jobs. Cardiac muscle, found in
the heart, is concerned with pumping blood through the circulatory system.
Smooth muscle is concerned with various involuntary movements, such as having
one's hair stand on end when cold or frightened, or moving food through the
digestive system. This chapter will examine the structure and function of these
three types of muscles.

## Sections

- **Overview of Muscle Tissues** — the three types of muscle tissue, and the properties of contractibility and extensibility.
- **Skeletal Muscle** — the connective tissue layers that package skeletal muscle, how muscles work with tendons to move the body, the areas of a muscle fiber, and excitation-contraction coupling.
- **Muscle Fiber Contraction and Relaxation** — the components involved in a muscle contraction, how a muscle fiber contracts and relaxes, and the sliding filament model.
- **Nervous System Control of Muscle Tension** — concentric, isotonic, and eccentric contractions, the length-tension relationship, the three phases of a muscle twitch, and wave summation, tetanus, and treppe.
- **Types of Muscle Fibers** — the types of skeletal muscle fibers, and the differences between fast and slow fibers.
- **Exercise and Muscle Performance** — hypertrophy and atrophy, how resistance exercise builds muscle, and how performance-enhancing substances affect muscle.
- **Cardiac Muscle Tissue** — intercalated discs and gap junctions, and the desmosome.
- **Smooth Muscle** — the dense body, how smooth muscle works with internal organs and passageways, how smooth muscle differs from skeletal and cardiac muscle, and single-unit versus multi-unit smooth muscle.
- **Development and Regeneration of Muscle Tissue** — the function of satellite cells, fibrosis, and which muscle has the greatest regeneration ability.
