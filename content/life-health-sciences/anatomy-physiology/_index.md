Anatomy is the study of the body's structures and physiology the study of
how they work; this book teaches them together, system by system. It is the
two-semester course for nursing and allied-health students: it opens with
the levels of organization — the chemistry of the body, the cell, and the
four tissue types — then covers support and movement (skin, bone, joints,
and muscle), regulation and control (the nervous and endocrine systems),
fluids and transport (blood, the heart, the vessels, and the lymphatic and
immune systems), energy and exchange (respiration, digestion, metabolism,
the urinary system, and fluid and acid-base balance), and closes with
reproduction, development, and inheritance.

## Chapters

### Unit 1: Levels of Organization

- **An Introduction to the Human Body** — an overview of anatomy and
  physiology, the body's structural organization, the functions and
  requirements of human life, homeostasis, anatomical terminology, and
  medical imaging.
- **The Chemical Level of Organization** — the chemical composition of the
  human body: matter, elements, and subatomic particles; ionic, covalent,
  and hydrogen bonds; how chemical reactions store and release energy; and
  the inorganic and organic compounds essential to human functioning.

## Planned contents

The remaining 26 chapters of Anatomy and Physiology 2e's six units will
appear in this order:

- **Unit 1: Levels of Organization** — The Cellular Level of Organization;
  The Tissue Level of Organization.
- **Unit 2: Support and Movement** — The Integumentary System; Bone Tissue and
  the Skeletal System; Axial Skeleton; The Appendicular Skeleton; Joints;
  Muscle Tissue; The Muscular System.
- **Unit 3: Regulation, Integration, and Control** — The Nervous System and
  Nervous Tissue; Anatomy of the Nervous System; The Somatic Nervous System;
  The Autonomic Nervous System; The Neurological Exam; The Endocrine System.
- **Unit 4: Fluids and Transport** — The Cardiovascular System: Blood; The
  Cardiovascular System: The Heart; The Cardiovascular System: Blood Vessels
  and Circulation; The Lymphatic and Immune System.
- **Unit 5: Energy, Maintenance, and Environmental Exchange** — The
  Respiratory System; The Digestive System; Metabolism and Nutrition; The
  Urinary System; Fluid, Electrolyte, and Acid-Base Balance.
- **Unit 6: Human Development and the Continuity of Life** — The Reproductive
  System; Development and Inheritance.
