The life sciences shelf holds a full college biology course and an
allied-health microbiology course, with anatomy and physiology being written
now and nursing and nutrition to follow.
Every book here follows the same rules as the rest of the library: openly
licensed sources, interactive exercises on every page, free forever, no
accounts.
