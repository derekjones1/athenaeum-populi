Oil from the chaulmoogra tree had been shown to impact the disease, but the substance was extremely thick and viscous, rendering it unusable as an injectable treatment. Ball recognized that the oil's ester compounds were critical to counteracting the bacteria, so she created a method to isolate the esters using alcohol, and after further manipulation created a water soluble version. Ball unfortunately passed away a year later, and the university president fraudulently published her work as his own; however, a local surgeon who had collaborated with Ball repudiated those claims.
