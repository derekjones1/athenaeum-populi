## Chapter 4: Stoichiometry of Chemical Reactions

### 4.1 Writing and Balancing Chemical Equations

{{< textin
  question="Write a balanced equation for the reaction of molecular nitrogen (N₂) and oxygen (O₂) to form dinitrogen pentoxide."
  answer="2N₂ + 5O₂ ⟶ 2N₂O₅"
  answerForm="equation"
>}}

{{< textin
  question="Special conditions necessary for a reaction are sometimes designated by writing a word or symbol above or below a chemical equation’s arrow. For example, a reaction carried out by heating may be indicated by the uppercase Greek letter ________ over the arrow."
  answer="delta"
  accept="Δ"
>}}

{{< textin
  question="When carbon dioxide is dissolved in an aqueous solution of sodium hydroxide, the mixture reacts to yield aqueous sodium carbonate and liquid water. Write balanced molecular, complete ionic, and net ionic equations for this process. Enter the net ionic equation."
  answer="CO₂(aq) + 2OH⁻(aq) ⟶ CO₃²⁻(aq) + H₂O(l)"
  answerForm="equation"
>}}

### 4.2 Classifying Chemical Reactions

{{< textin
  question="Predict the result of mixing reasonably concentrated solutions of the following ionic compounds. If precipitation is expected, write a balanced net ionic equation for the reaction: lead (II) nitrate and ammonium carbonate. Enter the net ionic equation."
  answer="Pb²⁺(aq) + CO₃²⁻(aq) ⟶ PbCO₃(s)"
  answerForm="equation"
>}}

{{< textin
  question="Write a balanced chemical equation for the acid-base reaction described here: a solution of barium hydroxide is neutralized with a solution of nitric acid."

  answerForm="equation"
>}}

### 5.1 Energy Basics

{{< textin
  question="The energy associated with an object’s motion, equal to one-half the product of the object’s mass and the square of its velocity, is called ________ energy."
  answer="kinetic"
  accept="kinetic energy"
>}}

{{< textin
  question="When two substances at different temperatures are placed in contact, heat flows from the hotter substance to the cooler one until both are at the same temperature. The two substances have then reached thermal ________."

>}}
