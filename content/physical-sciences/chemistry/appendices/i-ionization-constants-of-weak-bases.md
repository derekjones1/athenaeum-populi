**Ionization Constants of Weak Bases**

| Base | $K_\text{b}$ at 25 °C |
|---|---|
| ammonia | $1.8 \times 10^{-5}$ |
| dimethylamine | $5.9 \times 10^{-4}$ |
| methylamine | $4.4 \times 10^{-4}$ |
| phenylamine (aniline) | $4.3 \times 10^{-10}$ |
| trimethylamine | $6.3 \times 10^{-5}$ |
