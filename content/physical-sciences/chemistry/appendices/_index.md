The chapters refer to these reference pages by letter: “Appendix G” is the table of standard thermodynamic properties, “Appendix L” the table of standard electrode potentials. Each page carries the data of the book’s own appendix.

## Appendices

- **Appendix A: The Periodic Table** — the periodic table of the 118 elements, with every element’s atomic number, symbol, name, and atomic mass also listed in a table.
- **Appendix B: Essential Mathematics** — arithmetic in exponential notation, significant figures, logarithms, the quadratic formula, and graphing.
- **Appendix C: Units and Conversion Factors** — conversion factors for units of length, volume, mass, energy, and pressure.
- **Appendix D: Fundamental Physical Constants** — the values of the constants, from the atomic mass unit to the speed of light.
- **Appendix E: Water Properties** — the density, vapor pressure, and ion-product constant of water at different temperatures, its heat capacities, its melting and boiling data, and its absorption spectrum.
- **Appendix F: Composition of Commercial Acids and Bases** — the density, percentage by mass, and molarity of eight concentrated solutions.
- **Appendix G: Standard Thermodynamic Properties for Selected Substances** — standard enthalpies and free energies of formation and standard entropies, grouped by element.
- **Appendix H: Ionization Constants of Weak Acids** — acid ionization constants at 25 °C, with Lewis structures.
- **Appendix I: Ionization Constants of Weak Bases** — base ionization constants at 25 °C, with Lewis structures.
- **Appendix J: Solubility Products** — solubility product constants at 25 °C, grouped by metal.
- **Appendix K: Formation Constants for Complex Ions** — formation constants and the equilibria they describe.
- **Appendix L: Standard Electrode (Half-Cell) Potentials** — standard reduction potentials, listed alphabetically by element.
- **Appendix M: Half-Lives for Several Radioactive Isotopes** — half-lives and types of emission.
