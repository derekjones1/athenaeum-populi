These playbooks describe the repository's current Hugo authoring workflow and
the checks that protect source fidelity, accessibility, mathematics, and
interactive grading.

## Playbooks

- **Section Authoring Playbook** — author and verify textbook sections with
  Markdown, Hugo shortcodes, source-ledger review, and repository-wide tests.
- **Knowledge Check Playbook** — build non-overlapping cumulative assessments
  from source-book practice material and official answer keys.
