In this chapter, we will introduce two new types of functions, exponential
functions and logarithmic functions. These functions are used extensively in
business and the sciences.

## Sections

- **Finding Composite and Inverse Functions** — finding and evaluating
  composite functions, determining whether a function is one-to-one, and
  finding inverse functions.
- **Evaluate and Graph Exponential Functions** — graphing exponential
  functions, solving exponential equations, and using exponential models for
  growth, decay, and compound interest.
- **Evaluate and Graph Logarithmic Functions** — converting between
  exponential and logarithmic forms, evaluating and graphing logarithmic
  functions, solving logarithmic equations, and using logarithmic models.
- **Use the Properties of Logarithms** — expanding and condensing logarithmic
  expressions with the properties of logarithms and using the Change-of-Base
  Formula.
- **Solve Exponential and Logarithmic Equations** — solving logarithmic
  equations with logarithm properties, solving exponential equations with
  logarithms, and using exponential models in applications.
